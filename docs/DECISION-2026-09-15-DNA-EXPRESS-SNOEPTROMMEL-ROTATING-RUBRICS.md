# DNA EXPRESS — SNOEPTROMMEL / ROTERENDE KORTE RUBRIEKEN

Date: 2026-09-15
Status: ACCEPTED EDITORIAL DIRECTION / ACTIVE FOR WEEKLY PRODUCTION

## Ben's preference
DNA EXPRESS should combine substantial journalism with short factual discoveries and surprising reminders of artists, bands and the wider people around music.

Two short rubrics are weekly anchors:
1. **NEWS OF THE WORLD** — compact current worldwide music news.
2. **DEZE WEEK — DOOR DE JAREN** — compact historical events from the corresponding calendar week across multiple years.

All other short rubrics belong to the **SNOEPTROMMEL**: a rotating editorial reservoir. They appear only when the week produces something genuinely worthwhile. Do not mechanically fill them and do not run all of them every week.

## Milestones / time-awareness — new priority family
Ben specifically values facts that suddenly make the passage of musical time tangible.

### JARIG DEZE WEEK
Birthdays in the publication week, not limited to front-persons. Candidates include musicians, producers, engineers, managers, label founders/executives, designers/illustrators/photographers and other creator figures where the connection is worth mentioning.

Preferred form: name — age — role/connection — one compact reason this person matters.

Example form only: `X wordt 62 — gitarist van ... / producer van ...`

Never guess birth dates or roles. Prefer primary/strong biographical sources and cross-check surprising ages.

### HOE LANG AL?
Band/artist/project anniversaries: formation, first rehearsal, first named line-up, first release, first gig, label founding, venue opening, studio opening, magazine founding, etc.

Use precise language because 'band exists for 20 years' can hide multiple possible starting dates. If only month/year is known, say so.

### RONDE GETALLEN
10/20/25/30/40/50-year milestones for albums, singles, gigs, tours, labels, studios, venues, festivals, magazines, iconic artwork and other relevant music-world events.

Avoid anniversary spam. The number alone is not a story; select milestones with Music-DNA value or genuine surprise.

### DAT IS OOK AL ... JAAR GELEDEN
A looser one-line time-shock item for events that need not be round anniversaries. Designed for the reaction: 'Is dat werkelijk al zo lang geleden?'

## Existing rotating Snoeptrommel candidates
- **UIT DE BAKKEN** — interesting release/reissue/box/archive find.
- **VERGETEN?** — overlooked song, B-side, album or artist.
- **DE VERBINDING** — unexpected connection between artists via people, label, studio, tour, visual creator, venue etc.
- **ACHTER DE KNOPPEN** — producer/studio/recording detail.
- **WIE MAAKTE DIT?** — artwork/logo/mascot/poster/stage creator.
- **5 VRAGEN / 5 ANTWOORDEN** — compact extraction/synthesis from a strong new or historical interview; never fabricate an interview.
- **EEN NUMMER** — the story behind one song.
- **HET BEELD** — one documented photograph/image moment and its story.
- **TOEN ZE HIER WAREN** — documented NL/BE/DE/LU concert/archive find.
- **VANDAAR DUS...** — meaningful place: studio, club, street, record shop, rehearsal room, cemetery, town, etc.
- **WIST JE DAT...?** — a few verified surprising facts, not unsourced internet trivia.
- **TOEN & NU** — contemporary review/reaction versus later reputation.
- **BEN, PROBEER DEZE EENS** — one optional Music Master nudge outside Ontdek DNA; no rating obligation.
- **IN MEMORIAM / NOG EEN KEER...** — only when genuinely warranted.

## Additional rotating candidates

### WIE SPEELDE DAAR OOK ALWEER?
One surprising former/current band membership or session credit. Useful for musicians whose career crosses several Music-DNA worlds.

### OP DEZELFDE DAG
Two or three unrelated music events that happened on exactly the same calendar date, preferably across different years. The juxtaposition is the pleasure.

### TOEN WAS HIJ/ZIJ ...
Put an age on a historic event: how old was the musician when a famous album, performance or career turn happened? Use sparingly; often surprisingly humanizing.

### EERSTE KEER
First gig, first record, first chart appearance, first foreign show, first time in the Netherlands, first TV/radio appearance, first meeting between collaborators, first appearance of a mascot/logo/visual identity.

### LAATSTE KEER
Last documented performance with a line-up, final studio session, final show at a venue, last release before a long gap etc. Must be carefully sourced; 'last ever' claims require caution.

### VOOR ZE ... WAREN
Early names, precursor bands, day jobs or early musical roles before the familiar identity existed. Use only when genuinely documented and editorially relevant.

### DE MAN/VROUW ACHTER ...
A compact human spotlight on producer, manager, engineer, label person, road/production figure, photographer, designer, promoter or other important non-front-stage person.

### OP DIT LABEL
A small label story: surprising roster neighbours, founder, local scene role, catalogue connection, or why a particular signing mattered.

### DE ZAAL HERINNERT ZICH
One venue archive object: poster, ticket, programme, photo, setlist, review or anecdote, with provenance. Particularly useful for Netherlands and neighbouring countries.

### WAT KOSTTE DAT TOEN?
A historical ticket price, studio budget, first fee, record price or tour economics when strongly sourced. Contextualize rather than convert mechanically; avoid false precision.

### NUMMER 1 — MAAR WAAR?
A surprising chart fact from a particular country/territory. Use official/credible chart history and make the territory explicit.

### BIJNA VERGETEN NAAM
A producer, musician, manager, designer or label person whose name recurs behind several familiar records/artists. Can become a future deeper feature.

### ZELFDE STUDIO, ANDERE WERELD
Two very different Music-DNA artists/releases linked by one recording studio.

### ZELFDE PRODUCER, ANDERE WERELD
Two unexpected releases connected by one producer/engineer.

### ZELFDE PODIUM
Two surprising artists who played the same venue, preferably with a striking time gap or scene contrast.

### WAT GEBEURDE ER DAARNA?
One-sentence sequel to an old fact: event -> immediate next career consequence. Avoid hindsight mythology; source both sides.

## Editorial rotation rule
Every week:
- NEWS OF THE WORLD;
- DEZE WEEK — DOOR DE JAREN.

Then normally choose roughly 2–5 Snoeptrommel items/families only if the evidence pack yields strong material. There is no fixed quota and no fixed rotation calendar.

The same rubric may return in consecutive weeks if the material is unusually strong, but visual/editorial sameness should be avoided.

Birthdays and anniversaries are scanned every week as evidence, but they appear only when the people/events are relevant, surprising or useful reminders. This prevents a generic celebrity-calendar effect.

## Database implications
The Artist Information Network should progressively support date-aware fields/relations for:
- person birth date / death date;
- band/project formation date and confidence/granularity;
- membership periods;
- management/label/booking periods;
- release dates by territory where relevant;
- first/last gig evidence;
- venue opening/closing/history;
- label/studio/magazine/festival founding dates;
- creator credits and eras;
- milestone/event records with exact date, date precision, source and confidence.

Derived ages/anniversaries must be calculated from verified dates rather than stored as permanent facts, so they remain correct automatically each year.

## Weekly evidence-pack addition
Run a **calendar sweep** for the issue week:
- birthdays;
- deaths/memorial dates only when useful;
- formations/foundings;
- releases;
- gigs/tours;
- recording sessions;
- label/management/venue/studio/festival/media milestones;
- round anniversaries;
- surprising same-date relationships.

The editorial desk then selects, rather than publishing the raw calendar.

## Tone
These are not trivia for trivia's sake. Their job is to make the music world feel populated, connected and alive — and sometimes to trigger the exact reaction Ben described: **'Dat meen je niet... is dat alweer zo lang geleden?'**

## Principle
**Grote verhalen geven diepte. Kleine feiten houden de hele muziekwereld in beweging.**
