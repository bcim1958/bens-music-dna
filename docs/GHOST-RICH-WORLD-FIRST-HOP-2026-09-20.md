# Ghost rich world — first Verdwalen hop — 2026-09-20

Status: SELECTED, FACT-GROUNDED

## Decision
The first proof journey uses **Black Sabbath** as the first “Verdwalen” hop from Ghost.

This is not a generic similarity guess. It has two independent anchors:

1. **Ben’s own Ghost DNA** contains Black Sabbath — *Paranoid* at position 10. That establishes the personal Music-DNA relation already present in the Master.
2. **Tobias Forge himself** has repeatedly identified Black Sabbath as a major influence on Ghost and on his songwriting. In a Guitar World interview around *Prequelle*, Forge specifically distinguishes the Black Sabbath he draws from: not merely the heaviest early material, but the more harmonic, grandiose, orchestrated mid-1970s work, explicitly mentioning *Sabotage* and *Sabbath Bloody Sabbath*. In 2025 he again singled out *Vol. 4*, *Sabbath Bloody Sabbath* and *Sabotage* as long-standing milestones/influences.

## Why this wins proof v1
The shortlist was Black Sabbath, Pretty Maids, Therion, The Gathering and Queensrÿche.

For proof v1, Black Sabbath is the strongest documented hop because:
- the relation exists in Ben’s existing Ghost DNA;
- Forge provides a direct first-person explanation of the influence;
- the explanation is musically specific enough to teach something: Ghost’s Sabbath connection is broader than “heavy riffs” and includes harmony, grandeur, orchestration, progressive bravery and ballad-like scale;
- it creates a useful surprise: the route can correct the simplistic assumption “Ghost → Sabbath = just heaviness.”

No equally direct, first-person Ghost-specific causal evidence was established in this research pass for Pretty Maids, Therion, The Gathering or Queensrÿche. They remain valid Ghost-DNA neighbors in Ben’s Master, but should not be promoted to an explained causal hop without additional evidence.

## The listening bridge
Keep two different facts visibly separate in the UI:

### Personal DNA evidence
**Black Sabbath — Paranoid**
- This is the exact Black Sabbath track already present in Ben’s Ghost DNA.
- It proves the existing personal affinity edge.
- Do NOT claim that *Paranoid* itself is the particular Sabbath composition Forge says Ghost is modeled on.

### Influence explanation
Use **Sabbath Bloody Sabbath / Sabotage-era Black Sabbath** as the explanatory listening direction.
Forge’s own description points to the harmonic, grandiose, orchestrated and progressive side of mid-1970s Sabbath.

For the first prototype, a useful card can therefore say in Dutch, approximately:

> **Waarom deze afslag?**  
> Black Sabbath staat al in jouw Ghost DNA. Tobias Forge noemt juist de avontuurlijkere Sabbath uit het midden van de jaren zeventig een belangrijke inspiratie voor Ghost: niet alleen zwaar, maar ook melodieus, groots en progressief.

Then:
- button A: **In mijn Ghost DNA: Paranoid**
- button B: **Hoor de invloed: Sabbath Bloody Sabbath**
- optional deeper branch later: *Sabotage* / *Vol. 4*

This prevents the product from pretending that playlist membership and historical influence are the same evidence.
