# DNA EXPRESS-MAKER — Editie NUL — Art-direction blueprint

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: ACCEPTED WORKING BLUEPRINT / PRE-BUILD

## Purpose
Translate Art Direction 2.0 into one coherent visual composition before further HTML implementation. This is not a page-template specification. It is the dramaturgical score for Editie NUL.

## Governing test
**Salontafeltest:** would Ben pick it up, flip ahead, stop, return, and linger?

The edition must behave like a magazine, not a website. Navigation supports browsing rather than enforcing linear reading. Visual rest comes from composition and scale, never from empty generic white space.

## Visual score

### 1 — COVER — VERLEIDING
Dominant world: Green Lung / Necropolitan / occult winter London.
Composition: nearly full-screen hero composition; huge EXPRESS-MAKER masthead; very few cover lines; edition/date and `PRICE: 21 RATINGS` as authentic publication details rather than UI.
Image language: cemetery/occult London, raven, rail/Necropolis suggestion, winter darkness; band portrait only if it strengthens the image.
Text weight: very low.
Interaction: tap/swipe to enter; no web-menu rail.
Transition: cover image appears to continue into opening spread.
Reader reaction: **Wat ís dit?**

### 2 — OPENING + INHOUD — ORIËNTATIE
Dominant world: Green Lung still present but withdrawn.
Composition: map-like editorial landscape rather than contents cards. Destinations are placed into the world: Mijn Week, Green Lung, Prog, Hard & Heavy, Band/Genre DNA, LIVE!, Flashback, Dieptebom, Edelstenenkabinet.
Text weight: low/medium.
Interaction: destinations may be tapped directly; always preserve `terug naar waar ik was` behaviour.
Special: Dieptebom is visible only as `DIEPTEBOM — geen spoilers`.
Reader reaction: **Waar zal ik beginnen?**

### 3–4 — MIJN WEEK — PERSOONLIJK
Dominant world: Ben + Music DNA; publication becomes unmistakably personal.
Composition: one rich widescreen spread, not dashboard/cards. Muziekmeter, 21 official observations, notable discovery, emerging taste observations and reserve information become editorial typography/graphics.
Text weight: medium.
Visual device: data can become page furniture — oversized number, timeline, typographic strip, annotation — but never SaaS widgets.
Ontdek DNA: mentioned only as consequence/harvest-to-come; no duplicate playlist presentation here.
Possible `Van de MASTER …` only if there is a genuinely interesting fact.
Reader reaction: **Dit is míjn blad.**

### 5–6 — GREEN LUNG — ONDERDOMPELING
Dominant world: full Green Lung visual oeuvre, especially Necropolitan.
Composition: 2–3 screen-equivalents allowed. Large images, occult-London details, cemetery/rail/folklore motifs, text columns that enter and leave imagery.
Text weight: medium/high but paced.
Visual rhythm: dense and layered; controlled secrets/easter eggs.
Kanttekeningen candidates may be planted but not explained inline.
Reader reaction: **Ik ben ergens binnengegaan.**

### 7 — STEVEN WILSON — CONTRAST / SCHAAL
Dominant world: The Overview → Requiem For A Village.
Composition: radical reduction after Green Lung. Begin with black/cosmic scale, tiny Earth/perspective; progressively land in English landscape/church/village.
Text weight: medium; one large idea rather than many fragments.
Transition concept: **VAN HET HEELAL NAAR HET DORP**.
Important: this is not Green Lung 2; similarity in English darkness must be broken by scale, geometry, negative dark space and intellectual calm.
Reader reaction: **Hoe klein is alles?**

### 8 — HARD & HEAVY — ENERGIE
Dominant world for Editie NUL: Iron Maiden / Somewhere in Time 40 as editorial backbone.
Composition: original futuristic music street, steel, rain, signage, cables, posters, live energy. Supporting stories inhabit the street rather than appearing as equal cards.
Rights: never copy Derek Riggs artwork; translate visual grammar into original composition.
Text weight: low/medium, punchy.
GEZIEN?: deliberately plant only 3–4 meaningful references, not visual trivia overload.
Reader reaction: **KLABAM.**

### 9 — BAND DNA / GENRE DNA — HERKENNING + ONDERZOEK
This is not a 19-playlist radar and not a fixed dashboard.
Band DNA model: one namegiver + **29 around it**. Investigate why those 29 are there: musical kinship, personnel links, producer, label, management, tours, geography, visual/brand language, era and statistical peculiarities.
Genre DNA model: investigate what holds a 30-track/artists genre world together and where it fractures into subfamilies.
Editorial freedom: choose Band DNA, Genre DNA or occasionally Band DNA versus Genre DNA according to the strongest story.
Composition follows finding: network, family tree, map, timeline, statistical anomaly, one giant fact, etc.
Exact existing Band-DNA visual identities remain mandatory when used; do not invent replacements.
Possible `Van de MASTER …` is natural here.
Reader reaction: **Dat had ik niet gezien.**

### 10 — LIVE! — LICHAAM / VERWACHTING
Dominant world: people, stage light, movement, crowd, venue.
Composition: photography-led, potentially one near-full-screen live image with a tour-route/editorial trail rather than event cards.
Personal layers: `MIJN CONCERTEN`, `DICHT BIJ HUIS`, `DE REIS WAARD` may appear when useful, not as mandatory boxes.
Known Editie-NUL personal spine: 1 Up The Irons — Goes; KISS Forever Band — Vlissingen; Rush — Amsterdam; Thirty Seconds to Mars — Antwerp.
Local shows may receive equal editorial dignity to arena shows.
Text weight: low/medium.
Reader reaction: **Hier kan ik naartoe.**

### 11 — FLASHBACK — TIJDMACHINE
Window for Editie NUL: **6–12 September 1978**.
Editorial method: first open the actual music press of that week and identify a future significant artist/band who was then small, emerging or at a revealing early point. Do not select a famous name first and retrofit history.
Story form:
1. 1978 — what could a reader actually know that week?
2. `ZE WISTEN NOG NIET WAT WIJ NU WETEN.`
3. 1978→2026 wordingsgang: breakthrough, key records, line-up, scale, current activity, recent albums, audiences/sales only where reliably sourced.
4. Personal Music-DNA connection where data supports it.
Composition: period music-paper world ruptured by a visual jump to the present.
Historical facts must be date-boundary verified.
Reader reaction: **Toen waren ze nog dáár?**

### 12+ — DIEPTEBOM — VERRASSING / REDACTIONEEL KONIJNENHOL
Not mandatory every week. No subject quota.
Editorial trigger: unexpected relationship + enough depth + strong probability of immediate curiosity.
Music Master ritual: the editor may only press **FIRE** when the story earns it. `Finger on the trigger` is recurring internal visual folklore; do not over-explain it.
Opening: monumental water-column/explosion language or an original rights-safe translation; extremely little text.
After opening, the subject itself determines all visual language. No permanent Dieptebom layout.
Contents entry reveals no subject: `DIEPTEBOM — geen spoilers`.
Navigation explicitly allows Ben to cheat and jump here first.
Reader reaction: **Wat krijgen we nou?**

### 13 — ONTDEK DNA / EDELSTENENKABINET — BELONING
Dominant world: Music DNA's visual treasure chamber.
Purpose: not another 21-track list. This is the weekly harvest returned to Ben after 21 official observations.
Composition: dark, calm, precious; the actual weekly folder/playlist visual is a hero object. Track access remains practical but secondary to the visual/editorial arrival.
Content layers:
- weekly musical harvest;
- optional redactional `Vondst van de week` (not a new rating);
- **Het verhaal van de steen**: real geology/history/appearance/cultural use, then subtle editorial link to the week's music;
- growing annual colour arc.
System rule: the same weekly visual identity connects Spotify → Ontdek DNA → EXPRESS-MAKER.
Annual rule: 52 week visuals form a year rainbow; in the Annual they can become the coloured **corona around a total solar eclipse**. The 52 images should remain individually discoverable rather than being reduced to sterile pie slices.
Long-term: each year may become its own completed rainbow/corona in the Edelstenenkabinet.
Reader reaction: **Dit heeft de week opgeleverd.**

### 14 — GEZIEN? + KANTTEKENINGEN / ACHTER HET BEELD — ONTDEKKEN
`GEZIEN?` is a short playful **multiple-choice** visual quiz: `zag jij wat de redactie daar deed?`
Flow: kijken → vermoeden → multiple choice → onthulling.
Kanttekeningen/Achter het beeld gives selected context to meaningful visual references without cluttering the original art direction.
Not every reference is explained; preserve true easter eggs.
Quiz outcomes may later feed Annual statistics about visual recognition/engagement.
Text weight: low/medium and playful, never homework.

### 15 — OPDRACHT AAN DE REDACTIE + BACK PAGE — AFSCHEID
Short editorial curiosity signal, separate from taste model. A few candidates + free choice; not a survey burden.
Green Lung returns lightly so the issue closes where it began.
Back page is allowed to be extremely spare, but visually intentional rather than blank.
Possible close: `NEXT PRESS: 19 SEPTEMBER 2026`.
Reader reaction: **Wanneer ligt de volgende er?**

## Whole-issue dramaturgy
**VERLEIDING → ORIËNTATIE → PERSOONLIJK → ONDERDOMPELING → CONTRAST → ENERGIE → HERKENNING → LICHAAM → TIJDREIS → VERRASSING → BELONING → ONTDEKKEN → AFSCHEID**

Rule: **De volgende bladzijde moet visueel iets bieden wat de vorige niet had.** Different does not mean louder.

## Browsing behaviour
- The reader owns the route; do not force linear progression.
- Contents destinations are direct jumps.
- Preserve `terug naar waar ik was` after jumps.
- Dieptebom is intentionally jump-worthy.
- Mac uses widescreen/spreads generously; iPhone converts the same editorial hierarchy into one-column reading; iPad is intermediate and may support landscape spreads.
- Do not solve responsive behaviour by shrinking a desktop magazine into A4-like sheets.

## Interaction hierarchy
**Lezen → luisteren → verdiepen → ontdekken.**
Links remain visually integrated rather than blue-link clutter:
- track/album → Spotify;
- artist → Artist/Band World when available;
- source/original article;
- official site/background;
- setlist/tour/tickets when relevant;
- playlist → Spotify.

## Rights / provenance
Visual impact never overrides rights safety. For each substantial image/visual element record provenance and intended usage class:
1. licensed/rights-safe direct use;
2. editorial/reference only;
3. inspiration whose grammar is translated into an original composition.
Album art, logos and protected illustrations are not copied merely because they are visually ideal.

## Build gate
Do **not** turn this blueprint directly into a uniform page template. Before HTML:
1. create the whole-issue visual rhythm/proof from this score;
2. choose/verify rights-safe visual material and provenance;
3. recover exact existing Band-DNA identities before their production use;
4. research and date-verify the 6–12 Sep 1978 Flashback candidate;
5. decide whether Editie NUL genuinely earns a Dieptebom; never fabricate one;
6. identify the Editie-NUL weekly stone/week visual and annual-rainbow position;
7. only then implement the next coherent widescreen + mobile proof.

## Historical design note
Late-1970s NME is inspiration for editorial nerve rather than a template. NME's own retrospective describes its 1978 Slits cover as an arresting full-page visual before the band's debut album, while its broader history describes the early-1970s shift toward longer, more countercultural cover features. EXPRESS-MAKER should inherit that confidence in photography, story and surprise without imitating protected layouts or becoming period costume.
