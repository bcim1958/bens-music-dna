# Music DNA — State-aware workflow and equal-access rule

Date: 2026-09-18
Status: ACCEPTED IMPLEMENTATION RULES — RC1

## Core correction
Music DNA is the house. Ontdek-DNA is the daily discovery/measurement room, not the whole house.

The app opens into the state that is relevant now:
- unfinished daily work → resume the first unresolved set/track;
- daily work complete → completion state with optional entrance to Mijn Muziekwereld;
- full week complete → Saturday gift route;
- no safe active week/storage → truthful safe-wait state.

Closing and reopening never creates a new task or loses the current one.

## Daily interaction
A discovery detail is phone-first and centered:
real Spotify artwork when reliably available → track/artist/meta → prominent Spotify action → optional readable WHY THIS? context → four reactions.

Artwork is a guest inside the Music-DNA visual world, not the owner of the interface. If artwork cannot be retrieved reliably, the Music-DNA presentation remains intact without inventing a cover.

## Equal-choice rule
RAAK / GOED / TERUGKOMEN / NIET have equal-size touch containers.

The jewel inside each container may carry emotional hierarchy:
RAAK most exceptional/large → GOED → TERUGKOMEN → NIET most restrained.

This hierarchy may never reduce click/touch area or make a response harder to choose. Reward animation occurs after selection, not as coercive pre-selection animation.

Visible emotional captions under evaluation:
- RAAK — FULL HIT!
- GOED — STRONG!
- TERUGKOMEN — REPLAY?
- NIET — EXIT!

Stored semantics remain raak / goed / twijfel / nee. No storage migration is implied.

## WHY THIS?
The rationale is retained but collapsed by default to preserve visual calm. It must be readable on phone when opened.

## Completion
After today's resolved set:
- no forced continuation;
- clear completion;
- optional entrance to Mijn Muziekwereld;
- reopening later that day shows the completed state rather than recreating work.

The world entrance is not the Edelstenenkabinet itself. Mijn Muziekwereld is the parent space.

## Saturday
After the 21st official rating and all required reserve conditions, route to the existing guarded Saturday gift engine.

The future visual ceremony must remain short/optional and must never bypass fixed-21, positive-only, exact-Spotify, delivery-history or safe-wait rules.

If the gift is not safely available, the visual world must show a dignified truthful wait state rather than simulate a completed gemstone.

## Mijn Muziekwereld
The world must not collapse into a generic admin dashboard after a cinematic portal.

Destinations are spatially/equally represented. Ontdek-DNA remains visible as one room/state; Edelstenenkabinet and Muziekportaal are peer destinations, with later worlds added without demoting the existing ones.

Do not make a destination look functional before its underlying route is real.
