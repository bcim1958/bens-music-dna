# Decision — DNA EXPRESS-MAKER / Editie NUL

Date: 2026-09-13
Status: ACCEPTED CONCEPT DIRECTION — EDITIE NUL BUILD BASIS

## Evolution of the Weekbrief
The accepted Personal Music DNA Weekbrief is not discarded. It evolves into a richer publication form: **DNA EXPRESS-MAKER**, a personal weekly digital music magazine/newspaper.

Music DNA is the publisher, kiosk and archive. DNA EXPRESS-MAKER is a self-contained interactive weekly issue inside the Music-DNA universe.

Core line: **Jouw muziekwereld. Voor jou geredigeerd.**
Product north star remains: **Eerst muziek. Dan jij. Dan de wereld.**

## Purpose of Editie NUL
Editie NUL is a real readable proof issue, not a static design mock-up. It should test whether the subscriber experiences the Saturday issue as a pleasurable publication worth opening, browsing, reading, listening through and revisiting.

The historical W37 window is the preferred editorial test bed because releases, articles, reviews and events are already knowable and can be verified retrospectively.

## Publication model
- One editorial content model, responsive presentation for iPhone, iPad and desktop/iMac.
- Cover and contents page must feel like a publication rather than an app dashboard.
- Vertical reading is primary on phone; wider magazine-like composition may appear on larger screens.
- Published issues are archived by issue/week and remain historical snapshots.
- PDF/print may later be an export/derivative, not the primary product.
- No commercial advertising, advertorials or affiliate filler.
- House ads for Music-DNA destinations are allowed and encouraged when editorially useful.

## Editorial map
The existing **Genre DNA** structure is the primary map for recurring music worlds. Existing playlist/subgenre structure may provide finer rubrics. A world appears prominently only when the week contains worthwhile material; no forced filling.

Current Genre-DNA worlds include:
- Alternative & Indie
- Classic Heavy Metal
- Extreme & Modern Metal
- Garage Rock
- Glam Rock
- Hair Metal
- Hard Rock & AOR
- Prog & Art Rock
- Progressive Metal
- Psychedelic & Space Rock
- Punk & New Wave

The existing **Band DNA** collection provides personal editorial anchors and related-artist depth. Band-DNA anchors and their DNA relatives may generate weekly deep dives, archive pieces, current updates and discovery doors.

## Editorial ingredients
A weekly issue may draw from four distinct layers:
1. **My week** — 21 official ratings, Muziekmeter, positive reserve status, Ontdek-DNA harvest and playlist story.
2. **Current music world** — releases, interviews, reviews, tours, concert reviews and meaningful news.
3. **My music worlds** — Genre DNA, Band DNA and explainable related artists.
4. **Archive/context** — historical week/year, old music press, retrospectives, lists and enduring background.

Relevance and quality determine issue length. No fixed article quota and no filler.

## Digital-first interaction
Articles should be useful gateways rather than dead ends. Where relevant and verified, provide compact actions such as:
- Listen / Spotify
- Artist World
- Genre DNA / Band DNA
- official artist site
- Wikipedia/background
- original article/source
- setlist information
- venue/tour/ticket information
- weekly Ontdek-DNA playlist

Links must be purposeful and visually restrained; the publication should not look like a web link directory.

Editorial flow: **lezen → luisteren → verdiepen → ontdekken**.

## Core issue architecture for Editie NUL
The proof issue should contain enough variety to test the experience, approximately:
- cover
- interactive contents page / short editorial welcome
- Mijn Week / Muziekmeter
- Het verhaal van de playlist
- selected Genre-DNA worlds
- at least one substantial interview/background summary
- reviews / Wat zeggen de recensenten?
- Live! personal concert agenda
- archive/history or published-list feature
- Vondst van de week / editorial side door
- Ontdek DNA
- Opdracht aan de redactie

This is an architecture, not a compulsory page count.

## Dieptebommen
Two forms are accepted:
- **Redactionele dieptebom** — chosen by the Music Master because a current, historical or DNA-related hook merits deeper treatment.
- **Abonnee-dieptebom** — commissioned through the user's editorial request for the next issue.

A deep dive may connect history, current status, future plans, members, albums, archival journalism, interviews, live activity and DNA relatives, but only where useful and grounded.

## Opdracht aan de redactie
Every issue may end with a compact interactive editorial commission for the next issue.

Default pattern:
- Music Master suggests a small set of artists based on personal preferences, recent ratings/discoveries, Band DNA, Genre DNA and/or current editorial opportunity.
- Subscriber can choose one suggested artist or choose another artist freely.
- Optional focus may include whole story, current whereabouts, albums/music, members then/now, interviews/stories, or live/future.
- This is a **redactional curiosity signal**, not a musical taste rating. It must remain separate from RAAK/GOED/NOG EENS/NEE and the Muziekmeter.

## Music Master role
In DNA EXPRESS-MAKER the Music Master acts as personal editor-in-chief: searches broadly, selects narrowly, verifies, summarizes, connects and surprises. The user should receive the editorial result rather than the burden of source hunting.

The Music Master must not merely confirm known taste. Relevant side doors and unexpected but explainable editorial choices are desirable.

## Source and rights rules
The governing Weekbrief source rules remain active:
- summarize rather than reproduce copyrighted journalism;
- identify publication, author and publication date where available;
- link to original source;
- distinguish fact from reviewer/source opinion;
- preserve disagreement among reviewers;
- verify current/active claims with appropriately fresh sources;
- old material may be valuable but must be correctly dated/contextualized.

Personal/noncommercial use does not automatically grant rights to reuse press photography. Prefer permitted official/label imagery, licensed/open material, owned imagery, or source previews/links where appropriate.

## Advertising
Commercial advertising: **none**.
Editorial house ads may point to Music-DNA content (Genre DNA, Band DNA, Artist World, Ontdek DNA, archive issues) and may deliberately echo the visual language of classic music-paper advertising.

## Archive and future rhythm
Intended rhythm:
- weekly DNA EXPRESS-MAKER issue
- deeper quarterly Muziekmeter/trend reflection when evidence supports it
- annual special / DNA EXPRESS-MAKER Annual

Issues should accumulate into a browsable personal musical history rather than disappear after publication.

## Success test for Editie NUL
Editie NUL succeeds if the subscriber wants to browse the contents page, naturally moves between reading and listening, understands the editorial hierarchy, finds the issue personal without feeling trapped in an echo chamber, and wants the next Saturday issue.

The first proof should prioritize: **WERKEN → LEESBAAR → AANTREKKELIJK → SFEERVOL → RIJK AAN ZINVOLLE DIGITALE DEUREN**.
