# Decision — 2026-09-10 — Taxonomy repair closed

Status: **GESLOTEN**

## Decision
The broad Music DNA taxonomy-repair programme is closed. We do **not** pursue 100% administrative completeness.

The Master already provides a sufficiently large and reliable musical substrate to connect Ben's personal taste and to continue product/music development. Nearly 3,000 artist mentions have a usable taxonomy classification. The remaining difficult cases are accepted as an intentional residual category when resolving them would require disproportionate manual research, uncertain identity matching or interpretive guessing.

## Standing repair rule
- Evident and reliably sourceable -> repair.
- Reasonably sourceable without disproportionate effort -> repair.
- Ambiguous, obscure, composite-credit or otherwise research-intensive -> leave unresolved and accept as `mogelijk onoplosbaar zonder zeer intensief zoekwerk`.
- Never fill a taxonomy field by guesswork merely to increase coverage.

The unresolved remainder is therefore **not a blocking defect** in Music DNA.

## Reopening rule
Do **not** reopen the broad taxonomy-repair programme in later chats, audits or refactors unless there is a **very urgent/compelling reason**, for example a concrete Music-DNA function demonstrably fails or produces materially poor results because of the unresolved taxonomy cases.

A desire to raise the coverage percentage by itself is explicitly **not** a reason to reopen it.
