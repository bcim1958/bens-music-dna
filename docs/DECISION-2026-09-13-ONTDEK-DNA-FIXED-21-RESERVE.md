# Decision — Ontdek-DNA fixed 21-track gift and positive reserve

Date: 2026-09-13
Status: ACCEPTED

## Existing decisions preserved
This decision refines, but does not replace, the accepted separation in `DECISION-2026-09-11-MUZIEKMETER-ONTDEK-DNA.md` between the 21 weekly ratings, the Muziekmeter and the curated Ontdek-DNA gift. It also preserves the project rule that a weekly Ontdek-DNA gift is 21/21 checked tracks where the 21-track norm applies.

## Fixed gift size
Every weekly Ontdek-DNA Spotify gift contains exactly **21 tracks**. The gift size is not variable.

## Composition rule
The Saturday gift is composed in this order:
1. include the current week's positively rated tracks (`RAAK` and `GOED`);
2. fill the remaining places from the positive reserve;
3. reserve tracks must themselves have an explicit earlier positive rating (`RAAK` or `GOED`);
4. a track that has already been delivered in an earlier weekly Ontdek-DNA gift may never be used again;
5. `NOG EENS` and `NEE` are never used merely to fill the gift;
6. no unreviewed or invented-positive track may be inserted merely to reach 21.

## Reserve maintenance
The positive reserve is operational inventory, not a license to recycle old gifts. Music DNA should maintain sufficient fresh, positively rated, Spotify-deliverable inventory ahead of Saturday.

When projected fresh reserve is insufficient to guarantee the fixed 21-track gift, Music DNA should enter reserve-rebuild/protect behavior early enough to create additional **new discovery evaluations**. Only tracks Ben then explicitly rates `RAAK` or `GOED` enter the positive reserve.

The normal daily measurement remains 7 × 3 = 21 weekly discoveries. Reserve-rebuild evaluations are operational replenishment and must not silently alter the week's 21-observation Muziekmeter dataset.

## W37 recovery
W37 has 21 completed normal ratings but, after correctly excluding previously delivered W36 material, currently has 12 unique positive W37 tracks and no valid unused positive reserve. Therefore W37 requires a one-time recovery round aimed at obtaining **9 additional new positive, never-before-delivered tracks**.

Nine candidates are not equivalent to nine guaranteed additions: every recovery candidate still requires Ben's real rating. `NOG EENS` or `NEE` does not fill a gift slot. The recovery continues only as far as needed until nine additional `RAAK`/`GOED` results have been obtained and all 21 gift tracks have exact verified Spotify identity.

## Spotify safety
- W36 remains GESLOTEN and is never mutated by W37 recovery.
- The existing erroneous W37 Spotify playlist is not considered closed.
- W37 may be repaired/replaced only after the corrected 21-track set is complete and safely deliverable.
- Final closure still requires real Spotify verification.

## Product principle
**21 is fixed; taste is not faked.**

The system may ask for more discovery evidence when reserve is depleted, but it may never manufacture positivity or recycle a previously delivered weekly gift track to make the count look complete.
