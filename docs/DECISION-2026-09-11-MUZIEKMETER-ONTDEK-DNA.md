# Decision — Muziekmeter / Ontdek DNA learning layer

Date: 2026-09-11
Status: ACCEPTED CONCEPT DIRECTION — calibration required

## Core purpose
The Muziekmeter is a personal Music-DNA instrument. It never judges the objective quality of music. It measures how strongly Music DNA's selections resonate with Ben and, where applicable, what Music DNA expected beforehand.

The strongest first application is the complete weekly Ontdek-DNA evaluation cycle, not the filtered Spotify gift playlist.

## Correct weekly measurement object
Each Ontdek-DNA week contains 7 daily rounds × 3 tracks = 21 offered discoveries and therefore 21 explicit ratings.

The four rating states are:
- RAAK
- GOED
- NOG EENS
- NEE

All 21 ratings belong to the weekly measurement. The final Spotify Ontdek-DNA playlist is the curated harvest/gift and is NOT the measurement object, because NOG EENS and NEE do not belong in that final gift.

Therefore keep these separate:
1. 21 ratings = raw weekly measurement.
2. Muziekmeter = summarized weekly result.
3. Ontdek-DNA playlist/jewel = curated weekly harvest worth preserving.

## Weekbrief placement
The weekly Muziekmeter value should normally appear in the Personal Weekbrief. This makes it maximally current: after the final rating of the week, the complete 21-observation measurement is available for the Saturday ritual.

The Weekbrief may show, compactly:
- current weekly Muziekmeter score/state;
- RAAK / GOED / NOG EENS / NEE distribution;
- moving average;
- best and lowest weeks where useful;
- comparison with previous week / historical baseline;
- predicted versus measured result once prediction is sufficiently defined.

## Scale philosophy
The scale is personal, not objective. Ben's useful discrimination is concentrated toward the upper part because Ontdek DNA already prefilters candidates using known taste.

Working semantic zones:
- 0–69: SCHADUWGEBIED — outside normal voluntary listening territory; unexpected hits remain possible.
- 70–79: GOED — pleasant/credible fit, not special.
- 80–89: STERK — clear personal attraction.
- 90–94: BIJZONDER — notably strong resonance.
- 95–98: UITZONDERLIJK — exceptionally strong resonance.
- 99–100: intentionally rare / possibly unnamed top territory.

The exact numeric mapping of the four rating categories is NOT yet frozen. A naive equally spaced 4/3/2/1 average is specifically rejected as insufficient because Ontdek DNA already filters the candidate pool and weekly differences would likely compress too much.

Working calibration idea: RAAK must lift a week materially, GOED is positive but not exceptional, NOG EENS is unresolved rather than failure, and NEE is a meaningful miss. Exact weights must be calibrated against real data rather than guessed into permanence.

## 2026 calibration / reference set
W36–W52 2026 provides 17 weekly measurements = 357 individual ratings. Treat this as the first calibration/reference period.

Preserve per week at minimum:
- all 21 raw ratings;
- counts per rating state;
- weekly Muziekmeter result;
- moving average(s);
- best/highest and lowest weeks to date;
- predicted result if/when prediction is implemented;
- musical/selection metadata needed to explain why a week performed unusually well or poorly.

At the end of 2026, evaluate whether the formula creates useful spread between weeks. If scores cluster too tightly or behave counter-intuitively, recalibrate once using the complete 17-week set. The historical raw ratings must remain untouched so later formulas can be recomputed.

The 2026 series becomes reference material for 2027 and later years.

## Learning value
The Muziekmeter is not merely presentation. It is a learning layer.

High weeks can reveal which selection routes, musical relationships and forms of exploration create exceptional discoveries. Low weeks are equally valuable: they can reveal over-weighted relations, weak assumptions, excessive concentration, or deliberate exploration outside the safe centre.

Over time, useful signals include:
- weekly score trend;
- moving averages;
- frequency of RAAK;
- frequency of NEE;
- behavior of NOG EENS over time;
- prediction error;
- recurring characteristics of unusually strong/weak weeks;
- whether the engine improves without becoming safer/narrower.

A rising average can indicate learning, but it is NOT the sole optimization target. Music DNA must retain exploration and surprise. A week with some misses may still be valuable if it produces important unexpected discoveries.

Possible later companion metric: Ontdekkingswaarde, distinguishing pure weekly fit from the number/importance of genuine discoveries. Do not add this to the UI until it proves useful.

## Forecast versus measurement
The broader Muziekmeter concept may contain two states:
- VERWACHT — Music DNA's forecast before sufficient listening evidence;
- GEMETEN — actual result based on Ben's ratings.

Prediction should never masquerade as certainty. As real evidence grows, measured evidence takes precedence. Prediction errors are valuable learning events.

## Governing principles
- The Muziekmeter measures Ben's relationship with music, never objective artistic quality.
- Raw evidence is preserved separately from any scoring formula.
- Bad weeks are knowledge, not failures to hide.
- Do not optimize the adventure out of discovery.
- One unexpected hit from the Schaduwgebied does not imply a wholesale genre preference change.
- The score should be explainable from underlying evidence when Ben asks why.
- Hyperpersonal does not mean arbitrary: the system earns personalization through accumulated evidence.

## Product relationship
Saturday can form one coherent ritual:
1. Weekbrief — what happened in Ben's music world this week?
2. Muziekmeter — how well did Music DNA understand/reach Ben this week?
3. Ontdek-DNA jewel/playlist — what from that week was worth preserving?

This layer strengthens the existing product north star rather than creating a separate product.
