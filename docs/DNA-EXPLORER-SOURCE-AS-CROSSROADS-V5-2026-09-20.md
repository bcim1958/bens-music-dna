# DNA Explorer v5 — source becomes crossroads — 2026-09-20

New rule:
A useful external interview is not primarily an exit link. Explorer first offers a concise Dutch editorial summary, explains why it matters, exposes the most interesting facts/ideas as new doors, and keeps the original source available at the bottom.

First proof uses Guitar World's 10 May 2022 Tobias Forge / Impera interview.

Explorer doors extracted from that source:
- ABBA / songwriting / Spillways
- Fredrik Akesson / Opeth / Impera guitar sessions
- Slayer + Missy Elliott / Twenties creative concept
- Van Halen / Griftwood / Sunset Strip reference

Editorial guardrail:
- summarize and paraphrase; do not reproduce the source.
- preserve attribution when the point comes from the interview.
- distinguish a creative comparison from an actual personnel/influence relationship.
- original source remains reachable.
- source date and publisher remain visible.
- concrete claims still require grounding; playful Explorer narration may be light, but may not invent events, quotes or credits.

Mobile pattern:
- source + date
- estimated reading time
- punchy Dutch summary
- "Wat neem je hiervan mee?"
- "Volg een spoor"
- original article link last

Standalone prototype only; no W39 production state is read or written.
