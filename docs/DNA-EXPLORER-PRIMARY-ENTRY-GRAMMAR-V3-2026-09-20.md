# DNA Explorer — primary entry grammar v3 — 2026-09-20

At every artist node, three immediate choices precede the richer map:
1. Verken <artist>
2. Bands/artiesten zoals <artist>
3. ✦ Kies een interessante afslag voor mij

“Bands/artiesten zoals …” is not hidden under Relationships. It is a primary Explorer action.

Similarity is plural, not one opaque score. Candidate doors may be grouped by why they feel related:
- already connected in the user's Music DNA;
- theatrical/grand;
- melody + hard rock;
- dark but accessible;
- documented influence or songwriting affinity;
- unexpected relation.

A “like” door does not claim direct historical influence unless separately sourced.

Every selected similar artist becomes a full Explorer node with the same three primary choices. Thus similarity is an island-to-island travel mechanism, not a recommendation dead end.

The current Ghost v3 proof implements Black Sabbath and ABBA as grounded examples; other similarity families are visible placeholders until they have enough content to be fun.
