# DNA EXPRESS — Americas Source Expansion 1.1

Date: 2026-09-15
Status: ACTIVE SUPPORTING SOURCE NETWORK / EXPERIMENTAL WEIGHTING

## Purpose
Correct the underweighting of North and South America in International Source Network 1.0 and define a local-first Americas routing layer for DNA EXPRESS.

Core rule:
**The Americas are not one English-language news market. Search locally where the music, artist, concert, scene or historical event lives.**

This supplements `docs/DNA-EXPRESS-INTERNATIONAL-SOURCE-NETWORK-1.0-2026-09-15.md`.

## NORTH AMERICA

### United States — Priority 5 when artist/scene/story is US-rooted
The US is both a global news hub and a collection of distinct local scenes. Do not treat large national rock-news sites as substitutes for original/local reporting.

#### Core / frequent radar
- Revolver — heavy/metal/alternative; strong features, interviews, visual/editorial culture.
- Loudwire — broad rock/metal current radar; useful discovery/news routing, hard facts corroborated where consequential.
- Blabbermouth — very fast heavy/hard-rock news wire; high utility for leads and direct statements, but deduplicate press-release rewrites.
- Decibel — heavier/underground metal, long-form criticism and scene depth; use selectively where Music DNA overlaps.
- Metal Injection — metal/heavy current radar and scene commentary; useful secondary/current source.
- The Prog Report — US-based international prog/prog-metal/hard-rock specialist; interviews and current releases.
- Sonic Perspectives — prog/prog-metal/melodic/hard-rock crossover; detailed reviews/interviews.

#### Specialist side doors
- The Obelisk — high-value US independent source for stoner, doom, heavy psych and riff-driven underground; strong discovery route when Green Lung/King Buffalo/Elder-adjacent worlds are relevant.
- Angry Metal Guy — international review collective with strong US presence; adventurous progressive/heavy discovery; opinion layer, not factual authority.
- Premier Guitar — musician/gear/production detail, Rig Rundown and player context; use when instruments, sound or stage-world matters.
- Guitar World / Guitar Player — player/interview/gear/history layer; useful for guitar-centred stories and heritage acts.

#### US local-scene routing
For an artist, venue or historical event with a strong city identity, add city/regional journalism and archives before relying on national rewrites. High-value scene cities may include New York, Los Angeles, San Francisco/Bay Area, Seattle, Boston, Chicago, Detroit, Cleveland, Nashville, Austin, New Orleans and others as the story dictates.

### Canada — Priority 5 for Canadian artists/scenes; Priority 4 general heavy radar
Canada gets an explicit independent route rather than being folded into US/international English press.

#### Core candidates
- BraveWords — Canadian-rooted international hard-rock/heavy-metal platform; large current news stream, interviews, album/concert reviews and features. Particularly useful for classic heavy, hard rock, Canadian artists and touring.
- Metal-Rules.com — Canadian-rooted long-running metal source; interviews, reviews, live reports and valuable local-scene spotlights. Current coverage includes Canadian melodic/power/prog scenes and local Calgary/Montreal material.
- Exclaim! — broad Canadian music journalism; use for Canadian alternative/indie/rock and national scene context beyond metal.

#### Canadian scene routing
Search locally for Montreal/Quebec, Toronto/Ontario, Vancouver/British Columbia, Calgary/Edmonton/Alberta and other artist-specific scenes. For Quebec artists, French-language local sources may outrank English-language Canadian coverage for proximity.

## MEXICO / CENTRAL AMERICA / CARIBBEAN

### Mexico — Priority 4 when relevant
- El Cuartel del Metal — Spanish-language Latin-American metal network with Mexico and multiple Latin-American countries in scope; useful regional news/tour/interview radar. Verify original sourcing per story.
- Search Mexican venue/promoter/festival and local press directly for concerts, tour announcements and local scene stories.

Central America and Caribbean remain dynamic/story-triggered rather than assigned weak generic publications. Build country-specific source routes when an artist, festival, historical story or discovery warrants it.

## SOUTH AMERICA

### Brazil — Priority 5 for Brazilian/South-American heavy stories; Priority 4 international comparison
Brazil is a major independent rock/metal journalism and scene territory and should not be reached only through US/UK sources.

#### Core
- Roadie Crew — major Brazilian rock/heavy-metal publication with editorial staff, interviews, reviews, releases, articles, photographers and international contributors. Strong for classic/heavy/prog-metal, Brazilian scene and international artists. Its recurring Online Festival is also a discovery window into Brazilian rock/metal.
- Whiplash.Net — large Portuguese-language rock/metal archive/current radar; useful for discovery, history leads and broad current coverage. For consequential claims, trace to original source where possible.

#### Brazilian routing
For Sepultura/Angra and other Brazilian artists, festivals or local history, Portuguese-language Brazilian reporting receives proximity priority. São Paulo and other local scene/venue sources can be added dynamically.

### Chile — Priority 5 for Chilean stories; Priority 4 as South-American rock/metal radar
- Rockaxis — unusually useful broad Chilean platform spanning rock, metal, progressive/alternative material, interviews, reviews, live and historical features. Current site also runs explicit weekly local-discovery material (`Nuevos Sonidos Chilenos`) and has a magazine archive. Strong candidate for both current radar and FLASHBACK leads.
- Rockaxis Metal — dedicated heavy route within the platform for metal releases/news and international/local acts.

### Argentina — Priority 5 for Argentine stories; Priority 4 regional heavy/history radar
- Madhouse — Argentine rock/heavy publication with roots in the 1989 print magazine and an active online continuation. Valuable for Argentine scene, heavy-metal history, album analysis and original regional perspective.
- Add artist/city-specific Argentine press, venues and promoters dynamically, especially Buenos Aires, rather than relying on Spanish or US reporting for local events.

### Colombia / Peru / wider Spanish-speaking South America
- El Cuartel del Metal — cross-country Spanish-language regional radar, especially useful as a lead generator for tours/releases/scenes across Colombia, Peru, Chile, Argentina and Mexico.
- Country-specific sources remain a v1.2 expansion target; do not fill gaps with unverified low-quality outlets merely for geographic completeness.

## Americas routing by Music-DNA story

### Classic hard rock / traditional heavy / melodic hard rock
US: BraveWords (although Canadian-rooted), Loudwire, Blabbermouth, Revolver + original artist/label.
Canada: BraveWords + Metal-Rules.
Brazil: Roadie Crew + Whiplash.
Chile: Rockaxis / Rockaxis Metal.
Argentina: Madhouse.
Then compare with UK/Scandinavian/German/Japanese sources where relevant.

### Progressive rock / progressive metal
US/Canada: The Prog Report, Sonic Perspectives, Metal-Rules, plus artist/label.
South America: Roadie Crew, Rockaxis and local specialist routes when relevant.
Always compare with DPRP/Progwereld/eclipsed/Prog-Louder for major stories.

### Stoner / doom / heavy psych / riff world
US: The Obelisk becomes a high-value specialist side door.
Brazil/Chile/Argentina: use local heavy press for scene/tour context.
Compare with Scandinavian/German specialists when the artist originates there.

### Punk / post-punk / alternative
Use broad US/Canadian music journalism and local-city press rather than forcing heavy-metal sources. Latin-American local rock sources can be highly valuable for regional scenes.

### Live / tour
Start with official artist + venue/promoter. Then use the local city/country press for atmosphere, reception and contextual detail. A US/UK rewrite of a South-American show is secondary to credible local reporting.

### Historical / FLASHBACK
Prioritize digitized local newspaper/music-magazine archives from the city/country where the event occurred. Current retrospective articles are leads, not substitutes for contemporaneous evidence when exact-week reconstruction matters.

## Evidence observed during 2026-09-15 exploration
- BraveWords currently carries a very large hard-rock/heavy current-news stream and covers W.A.S.P., Def Leppard, Saxon, Van Halen and Scandinavian artists; useful broad radar but often close to announcement/press-release material.
- Metal-Rules shows direct Canadian scene value: 2026 material includes a Vancouver power/prog/melodic-metal festival interview and local Calgary/Montreal spotlights.
- The Obelisk actively covers heavy psych/doom/stoner and Swedish Greenleaf / US heavy-underground crossover, making it a credible specialist discovery route.
- Roadie Crew has active 2026 release calendars, reviews/features and a monthly Brazilian online festival that has featured more than 1,000 Brazilian rock/metal bands over its run.
- Rockaxis is active in 2026 with Chilean weekly discovery, rock/metal news, interviews, album reviews, live reports and historical features; its digital magazine archive extends back multiple years.
- Madhouse is active online in 2026 and explicitly positions itself as the continuation of the Argentine independent magazine founded in 1989.

## Geographic weighting change from Network 1.0
The old combined `United States / Canada = Priority 4` is too coarse.

New rule:
- United States: **Priority 4 general; Priority 5 when US artist/scene/history is materially involved.**
- Canada: **Priority 4 general; Priority 5 for Canadian artist/scene/history.**
- Brazil: **Priority 4 general South-American radar; Priority 5 for Brazilian artist/scene/history and relevant heavy discovery.**
- Chile: **Priority 4 regional radar; Priority 5 for Chilean artist/scene/history.**
- Argentina: **Priority 4 regional radar; Priority 5 for Argentine artist/scene/history.**
- Mexico: **Priority 3 general; Priority 4/5 when story/artist/tour is locally rooted.**
- other American countries: dynamic baseline; rise immediately with artist/story proximity.

Priority is a SEARCH ROUTING weight, never a reliability score.

## Weekly Americas exploration safeguard
When the weekly Music-DNA map gives a credible opening, the editorial desk should deliberately inspect at least one Americas source outside the obvious US mainstream route. Examples: Brazilian Roadie Crew, Chilean Rockaxis, Argentine Madhouse, Canadian Metal-Rules, or a story-specific local source.

This is a search obligation only when relevant, not a quota for magazine inclusion.
