## Source set A — app / UX screenshots (1–3 September 2026)

The reviewed screenshot series documents the evolution of the Music DNA experience. Durable intentions visible across the series include:

1. **Music first.** Listening and assessing music is the primary action; information supports rather than overwhelms it.
2. **Daily discovery rhythm.** Music DNA presents three carefully selected discoveries for the day.
3. **Listen before / while judging.** The assessment belongs directly to the listening experience.
4. **Four meaningful reactions.** The current semantic direction is **RAAK / GOED / NOG EENS / NEE**. Earlier generic heart/thumb/neutral/thumb-down imagery is historical, not a requirement for final iconography.
5. **Music DNA learns.** Reactions must have durable consequences for the taste model; feedback is not decorative.
6. **NOG EENS has its own meaning.** It means a genuine second chance / later reassessment, not a weak neutral score. The design exploration explicitly links it to a future Second Chance mechanism/list.
7. **Depth from curiosity.** A track can open natural paths to album, artist, story, people/band members, live/setlists, articles/reviews, interviews, media, related artists and other relevant context.
8. **Never get lost.** The user may go deep, but Music DNA remembers where they came from and provides a natural return route.
9. **Layered information.** Keep the surface calm; deeper information is available when curiosity asks for it.
10. **Personal music world.** The app connects discoveries, favourites/anchors, listening history, taste/DNA, news and relevant recommendations.
11. **Weekly gift.** A weekly Music DNA gift is a distinct recurring experience based on that week's discoveries, reactions, patterns and personal recommendations.
12. **Current-world layer.** Relevant recent articles/news, releases, concerts/tours and setlists belong in the artist/music world.
13. **Playback context persists.** Playing music should not make the user lose the artist/track context; elapsed and remaining time are both useful/desired.
14. **External attention signal.** Design exploration includes an app-icon badge showing how many daily discoveries still await a choice; zero means the daily round is complete. Treat exact implementation as design/product work until technically accepted.
15. **Verras me! is contextual discovery.** A surprise can expose era, country, style/mood, artwork/background, why it matches, and a route into the reusable artist world; it is not merely a random track card.

### Important historical progression

The screenshots include multiple visual generations. They are evidence of exploration, not a mandate to combine every element. In particular, the early green/black interface and standard reaction emojis/icons evolved toward a darker, cinematic gold/black visual language and bespoke reaction imagery.

## Source set B — reaction-symbol exploration

A dedicated comparison sheet explores four visual families: **Light, Music, Vinyl, Stage**. The later direction moves toward one coherent cinematic language rather than four generic platform icons.

Durable design requirement:

- The four reactions must be immediately distinguishable and emotionally legible.
- They must feel uniquely Music DNA and belong to the same cinematic visual universe.
- Current semantic labels are **RAAK / GOED / NOG EENS / NEE**.
- The final exact symbols are **OPEN / TO BE DESIGNED AND ACCEPTED**; do not treat any single exploration image as final without Ben's explicit acceptance.

Observed cinematic direction in the later mock-ups:

- RAAK: warm/gold, impact / spark / strong emotional hit.
- GOED: cool blue, positive but less intense than RAAK.
- NOG EENS: violet/purple, loop / return / second chance.
- NEE: red, clear stop/rejection.

These are design-direction observations from the supplied visual material, not permission to silently freeze exact artwork.

### Durable cinematic grammar

The opening material establishes a recognizable Music DNA motion/visual language:

**darkness / space -> point of light -> energy builds -> supernova / expansion -> portal / music universe -> musical artefacts emerge.**

Recurring qualities:

- predominantly dark/black environment;
- warm gold/orange light and particles;
- depth, glow, energy and movement;
- cinematic rather than generic app-graphic presentation;
- discovery as entering/opening a musical universe.

This cinematic language is relevant when designing the final reaction symbols and other signature Music DNA moments.

### Important boundary

The cinematic promo language does **not** automatically replace the established Genre-DNA family colour coding. Promo gold/orange and Genre-DNA taxonomy colours serve different functions and may coexist.

The Eddie Box-Open approved shot is a specific promo/story asset and must not be generalized into a universal UI rule.

## Source set D — Genre DNA colour coding

Separate screenshots supplied on 6 September show the intended Spotify Genre-DNA visual taxonomy: each main musical world has a stable family colour; playlists within that world inherit a recognizable visual family while retaining individual artwork.

Durable principle:

**colour = musical family; artwork = specific playlist/scene; Music-DNA visual language = shared identity.**

This is a functional navigation/recognition system, not decoration. New Genre-DNA covers should therefore not receive arbitrary colours that conflict with their parent musical world.

The supplied Genre-DNA overview also gives reason to audit whether every relevant Master category is represented. UK Punk is an explicit item to check; absence from a visual folder overview is a trigger for a Master coverage audit, not proof by itself that the Master content is absent.
