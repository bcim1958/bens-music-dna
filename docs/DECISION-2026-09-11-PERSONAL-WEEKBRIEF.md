# Decision — Personal Music DNA Weekbrief

Date: 2026-09-11
Status: ACCEPTED CONCEPT DIRECTION — FUTURE PRODUCT LAYER

## Core idea
Music DNA may turn the same living/current source layer used by Artist Worlds into an optional, taste-personalized weekly reading experience.

The Weekbrief complements — and does not replace — the Saturday Ontdek-DNA gift.

Saturday may therefore contain two distinct acts:
1. **Weekbrief — what happened this week in my music world?**
2. **Ontdek DNA — what has Music DNA discovered for me this week?**

A week of listening and rating can therefore culminate not only in the 21-track Ontdek-DNA playlist/jewel, but also in compact reading material selected for the user's own musical interests.

## Product principles
- Explicitly optional / opt-in. The user decides whether to receive/read it.
- Personalized from the user's Music DNA, Master, ratings, Band/Genre DNA, discoveries and grounded artist relations.
- Relevance over volume. A short week with a few worthwhile items is preferable to filler.
- Possible content types include new releases, album reviews, concert reviews, interviews, tour/live developments and genuinely relevant short news.
- No generic rock-news firehose.
- Information is offered, never imposed; deeper reading remains optional.
- Every meaningful item should remain a doorway back into the relevant Artist World, album, concert/tour context or original source where appropriate.

## International sources and Dutch summaries
Reputable international music journalism may provide valuable depth and context. Music DNA may offer a concise Dutch editorial summary of a relevant review/interview/article while clearly preserving source attribution and the distinction between source opinion and fact.

Rules:
- Summarize; do not reproduce or effectively translate the full copyrighted article.
- Name publication, author and publication date when available.
- Provide access to the original source.
- Preserve differing reviewer opinions rather than merging them into a fake Music-DNA consensus.
- A newly published article is a new source, not automatically evidence of a new/current artist state.
- Album and concert reviews are first-class source types because they can add context a music lover may not otherwise notice.

Potential service patterns:
- **NL Music-DNA-samenvatting** — concise Dutch summary of one source.
- **Wat zeggen de recensenten?** — compact comparison of several reputable reviews, preserving agreement and disagreement.

## Time/status safety
The Weekbrief inherits the Artist World time/status model:
- old is not the same as outdated;
- new is not automatically relevant;
- active/current claims require appropriate fresh verification;
- historical material must remain correctly dated/contextualized;
- deceased artists and inactive/legacy bands must never be mechanically presented through an active-artist template.

## UX role
The Weekbrief is compressed reading for a music lover, not compulsory homework and not a technical musicology feed. It should be pleasant to skim, easy to ignore, and easy to deepen with one tap when curiosity is triggered.

## Relationship to current development
This idea is durably accepted and should not be lost, but it does **not** interrupt the current Artist World 1.0 vertical proof. Current development returns to the H.E.A.T Artist World work after this decision is recorded.
