# Music DNA — iPhone home + DNA Express media split — 2026-09-20

## Home hierarchy
1. Ontdek DNA — today / daily ritual.
2. DNA Edelsteenmuseum — look back: preserved week journeys and musical history.
3. DNA Explorer — move outward: artists, albums, people, stories and side trails.
4. DNA Express — weekly editorial layer.
5. “Neem me ergens mee naartoe” — no-choice Explorer departure.

The museum and Explorer are intentionally separate full-width mobile destinations, not small side-by-side tiles.

## DNA Express: one edition, two presentation layers
The weekly editorial content has one underlying item model and two views.

### Large screen
Rich magazine edition: long-form text, large real artist photography, source material, interviews, side trails, video where useful.

### iPhone — Pocket Edition
Editorial summary, not a shrunken magazine. Each item can expose:
- title;
- 2–3 line summary;
- main takeaway;
- estimated reading time;
- “read more”;
- “follow this trail in DNA Explorer”.

The UI should call it “Pocket Edition” rather than “summary”. It is a deliberate mobile product, not an inferior copy.

## Media roles
iPhone: listen, discover, explore, quick reading.
Large screen: sit, read, view, experience the full DNA Express.

## Safety
This prototype is standalone. It does not redirect or replace W39 production routes and writes no production state.
