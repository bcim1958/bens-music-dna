# DNA EXPRESS-MAKER — Art Direction 2.0

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: ACCEPTED CONCEPT DIRECTION
Scope: Editie NUL and future weekly/Annual visual editorial system

## Core test
DNA EXPRESS-MAKER is not a website pretending to be a magazine. It is a personal digital music magazine that happens to live on a screen.

Coffee-table test: would Ben pick it up, turn a page, skip ahead out of curiosity, return, and linger?

**De editie is het tijdschrift. Het scherm bepaalt hoe je haar leest.**

## Permanent publication identity
- NME supplies visual nerve, surprise, immediacy and willingness to break the page.
- Melody Maker supplies depth, photography, authority, reading space and seriousness.
- Music DNA supplies personal intelligence and editorial relevance.
- 2026 supplies legibility, interaction, responsive behavior and invisible technology.
- Late-1970s inspiration is cultural/graphic attitude, not costume or template.

## Art direction before layout
Do not begin from cards, grids or reusable website components. Begin with the story, artist, image and desired page-turning rhythm. Computer precision should recede where controlled imperfection improves character: photostat/halftone texture, purposeful overlaps, selective misregistration, pasted strips, stamps, unusual crops and asymmetry.

Visual rest is created through composition and scale, not through empty white pages.

**Geen artiestenpagina zonder visuele artiestidentiteit. Geen lege pagina's; rust ontstaat door compositie, niet door gebrek aan beeld.**

## Three visual layers
1. EXPRESS-MAKER = permanent editorial identity.
2. Headliner = visual weather/ground tone of the weekly issue.
3. Article artist/subject = local visual language and temporary ownership of the page.

Album/catalogue artwork is research material for visual DNA: palette, typography attitude, symbols, textures, lighting, recurring objects, physical releases, stage language and photography. Protected originals are not copied merely because they are useful references.

Rights rule: **we lenen de beeldtaal; we stelen het beeld niet.**

## Visual dramaturgy
The entire issue is art-directed before individual pages are locked. Desired rhythm can move hard → quiet → unexpected → spectacular → intimate → hard. The next page should offer something visually different from the previous page; different does not mean louder.

Not every page needs to be spectacular. The issue as a whole must be spectacular to browse.

The reader is free to browse non-linearly. Contents/navigation must support jumping directly to a tempting story and returning to the previous reading position.

## Editorial restraint
**Geen tekst zonder redactionele functie. Geen versiering die zichzelf moet uitleggen. Vertrouw op het beeld. Een symbool mag een geheim hebben.**

Recurring motifs are welcome only when intentional. Avoid duplicated landmark imagery and avoid decorative cleverness merely because a visual joke is possible.

## KANTTEKENINGEN / GEZIEN? / ACHTER HET BEELD
Visual intelligence may itself become editorial content without cluttering the artwork.

Three levels:
1. kijken;
2. ontdekken;
3. verdiepen.

- KANTTEKENINGEN = selective visual footnotes/context where knowledge adds genuine value.
- GEZIEN? = playful multiple-choice visual quiz near the back of the issue; never homework or judgement.
- ACHTER HET BEELD = optional deeper explanation of selected visual choices and how an issue's visual world came together.
- Some easter eggs remain unexplained.
- Annual editions may aggregate GEZIEN? recognition statistics as a playful year-level insight.

Refined principle: **Het beeld hoeft zichzelf niet uit te leggen. EXPRESS-MAKER mag zijn lezer wél vertellen wat er te ontdekken valt.**

## Recurring editorial source worlds
### Band DNA
A Band-DNA playlist contains the namegiver plus 29 surrounding artists. It is not reduced to a dashboard of playlist names. It is reusable editorial research material for musical, personnel, producer, label, management, touring, geographic, historical, brand/visual and statistical relationships. Weak or absent relationships are themselves valid findings.

Possible forms vary with the story: network/family tree, DNA scan, one remarkable finding, chronology, map, or Band DNA versus Genre DNA. Exact existing Band-DNA visual identities must be recovered and reused rather than reinvented.

### Genre DNA
Genre DNA is an equally deep editorial source, but without one central artist. It can reveal clusters, borders, geographic or period bias, track characteristics, subfamilies and differences between genre kinship and band kinship.

Band kinship and genre kinship are deliberately not treated as the same thing.

### Van de MASTER …
The Master can supply personal editorial facts throughout the issue: durations, careers, countries, years, outliers, concentrations, unexpected relationships and other patterns. This is a flexible editorial device, not a compulsory boxed rubric.

## LIVE!
LIVE! should feel human, physical and immediate: people, stage light, movement, crowd, anticipation. Photography carries most of the art direction. Personal concerts and strong nearby opportunities can have equal editorial dignity to arena shows. Avoid generic concert-card dashboards.

## FLASHBACK
FLASHBACK is a time machine, not a nostalgia list.

Start from the actual music press/material of the corresponding historical week and look for an artist who was then small, emerging or at an interesting turning point. First reconstruct what could be known then; only afterwards move forward to what happened next and where the artist stands now.

Core dramatic idea: **ze wisten nog niet wat wij nu weten.**

The historical week moves forward seven days with each weekly edition, making this a renewable source of stories. Where reliable personal data exists, Ben's own point of entry may be added to the timeline.

## DIEPTEBOM
DIEPTEBOM is not a mandatory format. It is the editor's privilege to abandon the normal path for one unusually compelling discovery.

Qualification: unexpected connection + real depth + strong probability of immediate curiosity. If no story earns it, no Dieptebom is fired.

The Music Master has the recurring visual folklore of a restrained **FIRE** control / finger on the trigger. It remains subtle and should not become military cosplay or repetitive decoration. The opening may use the visual force of an enormous water-column/explosion as metaphor; the report itself adopts the visual language of its actual subject.

Contents may tease: **DIEPTEBOM — geen spoilers.** The reader must be able to jump to it immediately and return.

## Ontdek DNA / Edelstenenkabinet
Ontdek DNA is the musical harvest/reward near the end of the issue, distinct from Mijn Week.

- Mijn Week = what happened to Ben's musical taste and what Music DNA observed.
- Ontdek DNA = what that week yielded musically.

The Edelstenenkabinet is not merely a gemstone skin. It is the visual treasury of Music DNA across Spotify, Ontdek DNA, EXPRESS-MAKER, Mijn Muziekwereld and the Annual.

Each week has one gemstone visual identity. The same weekly visual should be recognisable in EXPRESS-MAKER and on the corresponding Spotify Ontdek-DNA playlist.

### Het verhaal van de steen
A weekly stone may receive a short editorial story: real geology, origin, history, visual properties, uses and only then a restrained connection to the week's musical character. Prefer factual wonder over invented mystical claims.

The 21 tracks do not need to become 21 literal gemstone buttons. One weekly stone/world can carry the whole harvest.

### Year rainbow
The 52 weekly gemstone visuals are designed so that the complete year can form a coherent rainbow-like collection while each stone remains geologically/visually credible. Do not falsify a stone's natural appearance merely to force the palette.

The annual collection can grow visibly one week at a time.

### Annual corona
A future Annual may use all 52 weekly visuals as the corona around a total solar eclipse: from a distance one luminous crown; closer inspection reveals the individual weeks. Digital interaction may open week/stone/playlist/issue details. Do not force the 52 images into sterile equal pie slices; preserve organic overlap and flare-like rhythm.

Concept line: **52 weken · één muziekjaar.**

## Editie NUL — visual dramaturgy
Current sequence to compose as one publication, not fixed equal-height screens:

1. Cover — Green Lung / Necropolitan: immediate seduction.
2. Opening + contents — withdrawn Green Lung/map-like orientation.
3–4. Mijn Week — personal proof that this is Ben's magazine.
5–6. Green Lung — full immersion in occult London/winter world.
7. Steven Wilson — space, scale and conceptual quiet; transition from The Overview toward the newly announced village world.
8. Hard & Heavy — speed/steel/urban-night energy; for Editie NUL Iron Maiden / Somewhere in Time 40 is the current visual backbone, without copying protected Derek Riggs art.
9. Band/Genre DNA — personal relationships/patterns rather than a dashboard.
10. LIVE! — photographic physicality and anticipation.
11. FLASHBACK — historical press world plus forward time-jump.
12. DIEPTEBOM — only if the editorial discovery earns FIRE.
13. Ontdek DNA / Edelstenenkabinet — reward, weekly stone visual, harvest and story of the stone.
14. GEZIEN? / Kanttekeningen / Opdracht aan de redactie / back-page closure, arranged for rhythm rather than mechanically forced into one screen.

Dramaturgical arc:
**VERLEIDING → ORIËNTATIE → PERSOONLIJK → ONDERDOMPELING → CONTRAST → ENERGIE → HERKENNING → LIVE → TIJDREIS → VERRASSING → BELONING → AFSCHEID.**

## Implementation gate
Do not turn these ideas immediately into generic HTML cards. First compose Editie NUL as a complete visual sequence. Then build responsive Mac widescreen, iPhone one-column and iPad intermediate behavior from that art direction.

Prototype wrappers remain proofs, not automatically canonical production architecture.
