## 8. Historical press / FLASHBACK — build an archive ladder

FLASHBACK needs exact-week evidence, not modern hindsight. Archive research therefore uses a ladder:

### A. Publication/rights-holder or institutional archive first
Seek digitized or indexed original issues from:
- NME;
- Melody Maker;
- Sounds;
- Record Mirror;
- Billboard;
- Rolling Stone;
- Trouser Press;
- national/local newspapers;
- radio/TV archives;
- libraries and national heritage collections.

### B. High-quality specialist transcription/index archives
Useful when original scans are inaccessible. They may locate an exact issue/article/date, but consequential claims should be cross-checked where possible.

Observed example: Fodderstompf indexes/transcribes 1978 PiL press including Sounds, NME, Melody Maker, Record Mirror and fanzines. This is a strong lead generator, not universal authority.

### C. Artist/fan archival collections
Use as leads to exact publication/date/page. Do not promote a fan transcription to primary evidence merely because the original is difficult to access.

### FLASHBACK evidence rule
For every candidate story preserve:
- publication;
- issue/date;
- what was knowable at that date;
- whether evidence is original scan, transcription, later citation or recollection;
- modern trajectory separately under EN TOEN…

Never let a later biography silently rewrite the historical point of view.

## 9. Source-network routing upgrade

The weekly production machine now treats source discovery as a graph rather than a list:

**artist/story origin → local-language source → specialist genre source → primary factual source → international comparison → archive/history route when needed**

Examples:
- Swedish melodic hard rock → Rocknytt / Sweden Rock → official/label → Rock Hard/Louder/BURRN! comparison.
- Mexican prog-metal show → Heavy Mextal → venue/promoter/artist → Prog Report/Sonic Perspectives comparison.
- Peruvian classic-metal event → RockMetal → venue/promoter → regional Latin-American comparison.
- Polish classic/prog story → Teraz Rock → official source → eclipsed/Progwereld/Louder comparison.
- 1978 Flashback → exact-week press archive/index first → corroborate → only then modern EN TOEN… layer.
