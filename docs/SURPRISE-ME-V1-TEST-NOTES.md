# Verras me! v1 — test notes

Test target: iPhone / mobile Safari.

Please evaluate only the interaction grammar, not final completeness of the catalogue.

Questions:
- Does one-tap `Verras me!` feel immediate and inviting?
- Does `Stuur mijn verrassing` stay optional rather than becoming a form to complete?
- Do combined filters feel playful rather than technical?
- Does `Waarom deze?` explain enough without slowing the journey?
- Does Spotify feel like part of the musical journey?
- Does `Verras me nog eens` invite another pull?
- Does the screen still feel like a musical resting place rather than a database interface?

Known prototype limitations:
- small sample dataset only;
- non-H.E.A.T artist-world links are placeholders;
- Spotify links are search links in this proof; production will use Master Spotify IDs/URIs;
- Mood/Theme sample is proof enrichment, not Master-wide coverage;
- recent history is session-only in v1.

Do not scale this implementation before the phone feel is accepted.
