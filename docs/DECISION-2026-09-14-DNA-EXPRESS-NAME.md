# Decision — DNA EXPRESS publication name

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: ACCEPTED

## Decision
The publication name changes from **DNA EXPRESS-MAKER** to **DNA EXPRESS**.

Working line remains:
**The personal music weekly · powered by Music DNA**

## Why
DNA EXPRESS is shorter, sharper and more autonomous. The publication no longer needs to carry its historical inspiration in its title. Melody Maker, NME, BRAVO and other formative music press can remain part of the publication's ancestry and editorial memory without becoming literal title components.

The word **EXPRESS** supports several useful layers without requiring explanation on the cover:
- direct / fast / priority: a weekly route through Ben's music world;
- expression: Music DNA gives Ben's music world a form and editorial voice;
- intentionality: the issue is deliberately selected and edited for Ben.

These layers are subtext, not a train-themed branding obligation. Railway language may appear sparingly when editorially apt, but must not become a visual gimmick.

## Masthead
Canonical wording: **DNA EXPRESS**.
Typography/punctuation (`DNA EXPRESS`, `DNA·EXPRESS`, `DNA/EXPRESS`) may still be explored as art direction, but may not change the publication name.

## Issue notation
Use week/year logic, e.g. **EDITIE NUL · W37 · 2026** and later **No.001 · W38 · 2026**. Do not substitute generated month/date labels.

## Cover/contents learning from visual gate
The recent image-first Green Lung cover/contents composition is a strong direction for spatial architecture, not a source of canonical generated copy.
- Aim approximately 65/35 headliner world vs editorial entry/contents on Mac widescreen.
- Contents numbers are editorial entry/rubric numbers, not page numbers.
- Avoid duplicated `EN VERDER` cover lists when the adjacent contents already performs that function.
- Remove decorative wisdom/citations without editorial function.
- Generated phrases such as `Rituals for a heavier tomorrow`, `stoner rock als moderne mythologie`, and generic Green Lung explanatory copy are not accepted facts or headlines by default.
- Contents imagery follows the rule: seek the artist's world before the artist portrait.

## Consequence
Existing historical files/commits named EXPRESS-MAKER remain historical and are not rewritten. Active production and future state/documentation use **DNA EXPRESS**.
